Viewpoint

This installs the Viewpoint app into /Applications/ViewpointApp on this Mac.

  • The app runs in the background and starts itself when you log in.
  • You use it from a web browser on this Mac, and from your iPhone.
  • If a copy is already running on this Mac, its saved password, settings and data are carried over automatically.

After installation, a Terminal window opens and finishes setup (1–2 minutes on first run). Leave it alone until it says Started!

This package is not signed by Apple. If macOS blocked it, you right-clicked it and chose Open, or allowed it under System Settings › Privacy & Security — that is expected.
